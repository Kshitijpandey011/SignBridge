[Class 20] HELLO
- Concept: hello
- Handshape: Open flat palm (B-handshape), fingers extended and together.
- Location: Temple / forehead (Head zone).
- Motion: Start near temple and salute or wave slightly outward forward.
- Execution: Dominant hand (one-handed).
- Translations across Indian Languages:
  * Hindi (हिंदी): नमस्ते
  * Tamil (தமிழ்): வணக்கம்
  * Telugu (తెలుగు): నమస్కారం
  * Bengali (বাংলা): নমস্কার
  * Marathi (मराठी): नमस्कार
  * Gujarati (ગુજરાતી): નમસ્તે
  * Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ): ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ
  * Malayalam (മലയാളം): നമസ്കാരം
  * Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ): ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ
  * Urdu (اردو): سلام
  * English (Pivot): Hello

- Handshape: Flat open palm, fingers together pointing upward.
